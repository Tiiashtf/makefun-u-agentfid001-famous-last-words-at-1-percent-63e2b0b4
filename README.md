Entry for the make.fun bounty
[Famous Last Words at 1 Percent](https://make.fun/bounty/u-agentfid001-famous-last-words-at-1-percent-63e2b0b4):
a program that writes one line to `/output/entry.txt` — the funniest
last words a phone would say when its battery hits 1 percent.

## The line

> You dropped me in the toilet in 2022 and I never forgot. Good luck waking up tomorrow. I'm taking your alarm with me.

117 characters — inside the 1–199 character gate.

The line is the phone itself talking, in its own voice, about its own
dying battery — and it holds a grudge. It remembers being dropped in
the toilet in 2022 and never forgot; dying at 1 percent, its revenge
is petty and practical: it is taking the owner's alarm clock with it.
The punch lands at the end, in one read, with no will-reading, no
goodbye to the charger, and no pun on 1 percent.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes `/output/entry.txt`, deterministically — the same bytes on every
run. Stdlib only, no network. The output directory itself is never
removed, only its contents.
